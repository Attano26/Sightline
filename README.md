# Sightline

A market simulator for the facade and fenestration industry. You describe a move you are
considering. It works out who reacts, what they do about it, and what that turns into a few months
later.

Built for a physical, specification led, project based business. Aluminium systems, glass and the
building envelope. Not for software, and not for consumer goods.

**Use it at [attano26.github.io/Sightline/sightline.html](https://attano26.github.io/Sightline/sightline.html)**

## Getting started

Open it. A short setup asks for your name, offers Google sign in, and offers a key. All three are
optional and skippable.

**Without a key** you are in demo mode. Prepared simulations are loaded and fully explorable, and
every one of them says on the card that it is prepared rather than generated.

**With a key** every answer is worked out from your own context. Get one free at
[aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com), then Settings, AI key. It is stored in your
browser only, and sent to Google and nowhere else.

## Installing it on a phone

Open the link in Chrome or Safari, then **Add to Home screen**. It runs offline after that, and
updates itself the next time you open it with a connection.

The launcher icon is fixed at install time by the operating system, so if it ever looks wrong,
uninstall and add it again.

## Syncing across devices

Optional. Press **Continue with Google** and your work follows you between devices, stored in **your
own Google Drive** as a single file this app creates. It can read and write that one file and
nothing else of yours.

Two people using this share nothing. Each signs in as themselves, and each file lives in its owner's
own Drive. Your Gemini key is deliberately left out of the sync, so nobody can spend your quota.

If both devices changed since the last sync, it stops and asks which to keep rather than guessing.

## Cost

A simulation makes one API call and uses **no web search**. Web search is the expensive part of this
API, at roughly one and a third rupees per query, and this app never issues one. A simulation is a
few thousand tokens, a fraction of a rupee, and free on Google's free tier.

## Your data

Everything lives in your browser, plus your own Drive if you turn on sync. There is no server in
this, and nothing passes through anyone else.

Clearing site data deletes everything. Use **Settings, Your data, Export all** now and then.

## What it is not

It produces exploratory reasoning, not forecasts, and nothing in it is market data. It will not
invent figures, and it will not answer what is true right now, because it does not search. Verify
before any operating, financial or contractual decision.
